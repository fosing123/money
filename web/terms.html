TIANA Terms of Use

TIANA is a personal bookkeeping and financial education tool. It is not a bank, payment institution, investment service or financial adviser.

Personal records

Numbers entered in TIANA are manually recorded notes. They are not live balances from a bank or Mobile Money provider and do not move money.

Backups

You are responsible for keeping your Google Drive passphrase safe and maintaining a separate backup. TIANA cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase.

Availability

Offline features rely on your browser's local storage and installed app cache. Clearing site data, changing devices or browser storage eviction may remove local records if you have no backup.

Use and eligibility

Use the app lawfully and do not use it to store credentials such as Mobile Money PINs, one-time passwords or bank passwords. TIANA may block an installation identifier that violates service rules, but installation-level blocking is not identity verification.

Before public launch

This preliminary notice should be reviewed and supplemented with the operator's full legal identity, contact information, governing law and applicable consumer/privacy disclosures before public commercial use.
