01 · PLAN

Build a weekly student budget

Start with income you can reliably count on. Write down food, transport, data and school needs before deciding how much is safe to save. Leave a small buffer rather than assigning every franc twice.

  • List expected income.
  • Separate needs from optional spending.
  • Set a modest, repeatable savings target.
  • Review what changed at the end of the week.
02 · SAVE

Turn a goal into a weekly plan

Give your goal a name, a target and a date. Divide the amount remaining by the weeks available. If the weekly amount is unrealistic, change the target or deadline rather than abandoning the goal.

03 · NOTICE

Find spending patterns without guilt

Record expenses close to the time they happen. Group them into a few consistent categories. At the end of the week, ask what surprised you and what one adjustment would make next week easier.

04 · PROTECT

Keep Mobile Money details private

Never share your PIN, one-time password or recovery codes. Verify the recipient and amount before confirming a transfer. Contact your provider through its official support channel if something looks suspicious.
