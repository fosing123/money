01 · START SIMPLE

Build a student budget that actually works

Start with money you can realistically count on. Set aside essentials first, give savings a purpose, and leave a small buffer for surprises.

A simple weekly method

  1. Write down your reliable weekly income.
  2. List food, transport, data and school costs.
  3. Choose a realistic savings amount, even if it is small.
  4. Keep an emergency buffer instead of budgeting every franc twice.
02 · SAVE WITH PURPOSE

Turn a savings goal into a plan

A target feels easier when it has a name, a deadline and a repeatable contribution.

Try this calculation

Divide the amount you still need by the number of weeks remaining. If that weekly amount is too high, extend the date or reduce the target. A realistic plan beats an abandoned one.

03 · SPEND WITH AWARENESS

Understand where your money goes

Record expenses close to when they happen. Group them into a few useful categories and review the pattern weekly, without judging yourself for one difficult day.

A useful weekly check-in

  • What cost more than expected?
  • Which expense was necessary?
  • What can I adjust next week?
04 · STAY SAFE

Protect your Mobile Money and accounts

Never share your PIN, one-time password or account recovery codes. Verify the recipient before confirming a transfer, and contact your provider through its official support channel when something looks wrong.
