01 · START SIMPLE

Build a student budget that actually works

Start with money you can realistically count on. Set aside essentials first, give savings a purpose, and leave a small buffer for surprises.

A simple weekly method

  1. Write down your reliable weekly income.
  2. List food, transport, data and school costs.
  3. Choose a realistic savings amount, even if it is small.
  4. Keep an emergency buffer instead of budgeting every franc twice.
02 · SAVE WITH PURPOSE

Turn a savings goal into a plan

A target feels easier when it has a name, a deadline and a repeatable contribution.

Try this calculation

Divide the amount you still need by the number of weeks remaining. If that weekly amount is too high, extend the date or reduce the target. A realistic plan beats an abandoned one.

03 · SPEND WITH AWARENESS

Understand where your money goes

Record expenses close to when they happen. Group them into a few useful categories and review the pattern weekly, without judging yourself for one difficult day.

A useful weekly check-in

  • What cost more than expected?
  • Which expense was necessary?
  • What can I adjust next week?
04 · STAY SAFE

Protect your Mobile Money and accounts

Never share your PIN, one-time password or account recovery codes. Verify the recipient before confirming a transfer, and contact your provider through its official support channel when something looks wrong.

05 · PLAN FOR CHANGE

Make a plan when your income changes

Part-time work, allowances and small business income can vary. Build your basic plan around the amount you can usually rely on. Treat extra income as a chance to catch up on a priority, add to a buffer or move a goal forward—not as money that must all be spent.

When extra money arrives

  • Cover an essential bill that is due soon.
  • Top up a small emergency reserve.
  • Put a realistic amount towards one goal.
  • Keep a record of what remains available.
06 · REVIEW WITHOUT JUDGEMENT

Give your money ten minutes each week

A short review can be more useful than a complicated plan you never revisit. Look at the transactions you recorded, check which categories changed and compare your actual spending with the limits you set.

Ask yourself

  • Did I forget to record anything?
  • What expense surprised me?
  • What should I protect first next week?
  • Is my savings target still realistic?
07 · PREPARE A BUFFER

Start an emergency reserve at your own pace

An emergency buffer is money kept for an unexpected essential cost, not for routine shopping. Start with a small target that fits your situation, keep it separate in your plan and build it gradually. Avoid setting a target that forces you to miss food, transport or school costs.

Keep the first milestone simple

Choose one realistic amount, decide where it will be kept and review it each week. Increase the target only when the first step feels manageable.
