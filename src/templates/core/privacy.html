YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

How TIANA handles information when you create an account and use its money-management features.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, TIANA may collect account details such as your name, email address and profile image; financial records such as transaction dates, amounts, categories and descriptions; budgets and savings goals; and preferences you share to personalise the experience.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage your account, display your own financial records, calculate budgets and goal progress, provide requested analysis, maintain the service, protect accounts and respond to operational issues.

3. AI-assisted analysis

When you request an AI-assisted analysis, TIANA may send a limited financial summary relevant to that request and the coaching preferences you chose to Google's Gemini API. This can include transaction or category totals, budget and savings-goal figures, and the ambitions or future plans you entered. The AI response is used to explain the information; it does not authorise payments or move money.

Do not enter bank passwords, Mobile Money PINs, one-time passwords, recovery codes or other authentication secrets into transaction descriptions or personalisation fields.

4. Service providers

TIANA relies on service providers to host and operate parts of the service. These may include Cloudflare for application delivery and storage, a managed database provider for account records, Google for AI processing and advertising where enabled, and Firebase for push-notification infrastructure. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services.

5. Cookies, sessions and advertising

TIANA uses essential browser storage and session cookies to keep you signed in, protect forms and support core functionality. Public pages may display Google AdSense advertising when advertising is enabled. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies in connection with ads, subject to the settings and policies that apply to those services.

6. Security and retention

TIANA uses account authentication, access controls and encrypted connections on supported deployments. No online service can promise absolute security. Account records are retained while needed to operate the service and for legitimate security, operational or legal requirements. Retention periods may depend on the data type and applicable requirements.

7. Your choices

You can review and update profile information and AI-personalisation preferences through the app. For questions or a request concerning your information, contact the TIANA operator through the support channel provided with your account or on the official service website.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as TIANA's features and service providers change. The date at the top indicates the latest published revision. Please review this page periodically.

This policy is a product privacy notice and should be reviewed by the site operator and qualified legal adviser before it is treated as a final legal instrument.
