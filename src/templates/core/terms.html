Basic terms for using TIANA's money-management tools and educational content.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. About the service

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details private, providing accurate account information and activity carried out through your account. Tell the service operator promptly if you suspect unauthorised access. Do not share passwords, PINs, one-time passwords or recovery codes with anyone.

3. Financial records and decisions

You are responsible for the accuracy of the amounts, dates, descriptions and other details you enter. TIANA's calculations and summaries are based on recorded information and may be incomplete if entries are missing, delayed or incorrect.

4. AI and educational information

AI-generated explanations and Money School content are general educational information, not personalised investment, legal, tax, credit or professional financial advice. AI outputs can be incomplete or wrong. Check important information and make your own decisions. TIANA does not promise a particular saving, investment return or financial result.

5. Payments and account balances

Displayed balances and goal contributions reflect records maintained inside TIANA. A recorded contribution or transfer within the app should not be interpreted as proof that money moved between a bank or Mobile Money provider unless an authorised payment integration explicitly confirms it.

6. Acceptable use

Use TIANA lawfully and do not attempt to access another person's account or records, interfere with the service, upload malicious content, or misuse the service to deceive or harm others.

7. Availability and changes

TIANA is provided on an evolving basis. Features may be updated, interrupted or withdrawn to maintain, secure or improve the service. Keep your own important financial records and do not rely on TIANA as your only record or emergency access method.

8. Contact and updates

For questions about these terms, contact the TIANA operator through the support channel provided with your account or on the official service website. Updated terms will be published on this page.

These terms are a working product notice and should be reviewed by the site operator and qualified legal adviser before being treated as final legal terms.
